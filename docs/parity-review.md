# Prototype parity review (roadmap step 115)

- Date: 2026-10-16
- Scope: the 19 requirement areas of `docs/requirements.md` (REQ-01 to REQ-19), storefront and admin, against `docs/prototypes/*.html`, including mobile, empty, error, loading and permission states.
- **Method.** Two read-only code inventories (storefront and admin) mapped each prototype symbol (`docs/prototype-map.md`) to a route or component, with its states. The main claims were checked again by hand. The browser passes of steps 54–113 (`scripts/browser-qa/`) supply the rendered evidence at 360–390px.
- **Verdicts:**
  - **DONE:** built.
  - **GAP:** the prototype has it and we do not; listed in the register below.
  - **OUT:** deliberately excluded, with the source.
  - **WAITING:** blocked on a business input.

Prototype demo code (`rng`, `mkOrder`, `simulateOrder`, `loginAs`, `applyDev`, guided tours, fake charts, demo reviews, demo coupons) is out by `CLAUDE.md` and is not listed again below.
